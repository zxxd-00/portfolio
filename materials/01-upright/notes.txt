summary: The upright carries the hub bearings, brake caliper and suspension pickups, and reacts every braking, cornering and bump load from the tire. I compared aluminum, steel and titanium on strength-to-weight and stiffness, validated the design with FEA, and planned its manufacture by CNC machining. Less upright mass means less unsprung mass, so the tire stays in better contact with the road.

[problem]
Every load from the tire passes through the upright: braking, cornering and bumps. It has to stay stiff enough to hold wheel alignment under those loads, and every gram it carries is unsprung mass the suspension has to control.

[requirements]
- Carry braking, cornering and bump loads
- Hold wheel alignment under load
- Package inside the wheel with brake clearance
- Manufacturable by CNC machining
