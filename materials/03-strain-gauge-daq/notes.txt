summary: An ESP32-based strain gauge data acquisition system that measures axial force in the suspension rods during track testing. Calibrated on an Instron and synced with the car's own sensor data, it gives the team measured load cases to validate suspension parts that were previously designed to estimated loads.

[problem]
Suspension parts are designed to calculated loads, with no measured data to check them against. This system records the actual force in the suspension rods while the car is driven hard.

[requirements]
- Measure rod force in tension and compression
- Log with no input from the driver
- Run from its own 5 V battery
- Clamp to the chassis without drilling
- Quick-disconnect sealed connectors
- Survive vibration and heat
