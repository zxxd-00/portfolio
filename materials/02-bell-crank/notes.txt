[problem]
The bell crank links the pushrod to the damper. Its geometry sets the suspension's motion ratio, and it has to carry the pushrod load without flexing while staying as light as possible.

[requirements]
- Set the motion ratio between pushrod and damper
- Fit between the chassis tubes
- Carry the pushrod load with margin
- Keep mass to a minimum

[hand-calcs]
label: Topology optimization
A topology optimization in Z88Arion showed where material was doing work and where it could come out. That result shaped the cutouts in the final bell crank.
